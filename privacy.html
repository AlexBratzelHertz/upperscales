Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

UpperScales collects no data about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device.

Deutsche Fassung

Who is responsible

Alex Bratzel, Germany
E-mail: lex2go16@gmail.com

Microphone

The tuner, Flow, Live Check, chord trainer, amp, looper, tab writer and songwriting listen through the microphone. The sound is analysed only on your device and never transmitted. Recordings from the looper and missions stay on the device and only leave it if you share them yourself.

Speech recognition

In Songwriting you can dictate lyrics or recite them from memory. The app uses iOS speech recognition for this and requires it to run on the device: neither the sound nor the text is sent to me or to Apple. Speech recognition only starts when you switch it on in Songwriting.

What is stored on your device

Your name in the app, your profile picture, your progress (applause, streak, stickers, best scores, lessons), your own tabs, saved songs and amp settings are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone. You choose the profile picture with the iOS photo picker: the app receives only the one photo you tap, with no access to your photo library. All of this is part of the device backup you set up with Apple (for example iCloud Backup), where Apple's terms apply.

Contact card

You can take your name and picture from a contact card. iOS shows the picker, not the app: the app receives only the one card you tap and has no access to your address book.

Camera, videos, files and Bluetooth

The Studio records you with the camera and the audio input you choose. Videos stay in the app's storage and only leave the device if you share them or save them to your photo library. Amp models and cabinets you open from the Files app are copied into the app and stay on the device. Bluetooth is used only when you pair a MIDI footswitch or pedal in the amp.

Internet connections

The app connects to the internet only when you use one of these functions:

  • Web search for a song opens a search on DuckDuckGo in Safari. The DuckDuckGo privacy policy applies.
  • Links to other apps, such as a backing track in the YouTube app, open that app. Its own privacy policy applies there.

Without them the app works entirely offline. The legal basis for these connections is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: they are needed to provide the function you asked for.

Purchases

The app sells nothing and shows no advertising.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. Profile → Reset progress deletes all stored data at once; deleting the app removes it as well. You can export a backup file under Profile → Data & legal.

Permissions

Microphone, camera, speech recognition and Bluetooth are only used after you allow them. You can withdraw any permission at any time in the iPhone Settings under “UpperScales”.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs. The pages set no cookies, use no analytics and load nothing from other servers. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: a website cannot be delivered securely without these logs. For transfers to the USA, GitHub relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Because the app transmits no personal data to me, I hold no data about you. Everything the app stores is under your control on your device.

Children

The app is for everyone who wants to learn guitar and collects no data from children either.

Changes

If the app changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before the new version is released, with a new date at the top.
