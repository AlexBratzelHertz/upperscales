Help & support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Write to me – I read every message.

lex2go16@gmail.com

Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version. It helps to reproduce the problem.

The microphone hears nothing

Open the iPhone Settings, then “UpperScales”, and allow access to the microphone. Play in a quiet room with the iPhone about an arm's length from the guitar. With headphones on, the microphone does not hear the app itself.

Notes are detected wrongly

Tune the guitar with the app's tuner first. Mute strings you are not playing. With an electric guitar and no amp, move closer to the iPhone.

I can't find the amp or the looper

The app grows with you: at first it shows the essentials, and the Rig tab follows once you're ready. To see everything at once: Profile → Settings → View → “Everything”.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your progress is part of your iPhone backup. You can also create a backup file in the app under Profile → Data & legal and load it on the new device.

Deleting your data

Profile → Reset progress deletes everything the app has stored. Removing the app from your iPhone removes its data, too. There is no account and no copy anywhere else.
